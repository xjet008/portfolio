Intro

Hands dirty. Timeline quiet.

I started my crypto and Web3 journey back in 2019 when I first started trading BCH. At that time I was mostly just curious. I wanted to see how all of this worked.

Later I started mining different coins like Monero, Cardano, and a few others — mostly for fun, and to learn how mining actually works. From there I slowly got deeper into Web3 and started exploring different projects and ecosystems.

I participated in projects like Monad, Movement, Story Protocol, Xion, 0G Labs, ZetaChain, Omni Network, Plume, and a lot of other L1s and L2s whose names I probably don’t even remember anymore.

I’ve also spent a lot of time on the technical side. I’ve run different types of nodes, including verifier and validator nodes, and tested nodes on pretty much every kind of hardware I could get my hands on. Old PCs, laptops, dedicated machines, servers, shared hardware setups — if I had access to the hardware, I probably tried running something on it.

Most of this was done on Linux. I’ve spent a lot of time messing around with servers, configurations, storage, networking, and different infrastructure setups just to see what works and understand how everything actually runs.

Most of my activity has been pretty low-key and happens on Discord. I’m not really the type to post about everything I do on CT. A lot of the things I’ve explored, tested, or worked on have just been happening quietly in different communities.

Basically I’ve done a bit of everything over the years — trading, mining, running nodes, testing hardware, Linux and servers, experimenting with infrastructure, and exploring all kinds of L1 and L2 ecosystems.

For me it was always more about curiosity and learning. I wanted to explore Web3 properly, understand how things work behind the scenes, and get my hands dirty instead of only watching from the outside.
