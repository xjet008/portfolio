Story

      
I started poking at crypto in 2019. BCH was the first thing I touched — not a trading arc, just curiosity with extra steps. Charts put me to sleep. I would rather doomscroll, break a Linux box, or reread a doc I already forgot.

      
I forget important things and remember useless shit. That is not a bug report. That is the operating system.

      
Mining Monero and Cardano was never a business plan. Then it was nodes: verifier, validator, old PCs, laptops, dedicated boxes, shared iron. If it booted Linux, it got a job. If it didn’t, I yelled at it — or yelled gib wl gib wl into Discord for sport.

      
Most of the work never hits CT. I pick a project and go all in. I hold to zero more often than I should admit. Fun is the fuel. Profit is the rumor.
