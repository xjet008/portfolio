// story, lightly roasted

        
I started poking at crypto in 2019. BCH was the first thing I touched — not a trading arc, just curiosity with extra steps. Charts put me to sleep. I would rather doomscroll, break a Linux box, or reread a doc I already forgot.

        
I forget important things and remember useless shit. That is not a bug report. That is the operating system.

        
Mining Monero and Cardano was never a business plan. It was an excuse to learn how blocks get made while the room got warmer. Then it was nodes. Verifier, validator, leftover PCs, laptops, dedicated boxes, shared iron. If it booted Linux, it got a job. If it didn’t, I yelled at it until it did, or I yelled gib wl gib wl into Discord for sport.

        
Most of the work never hits CT. I live in servers and Discord rooms. I pick a project and go all in until the joke runs out or the node does. I hold to zero more often than I should admit. Fun is the fuel. Profit is the rumor.

        
Monad, Movement, Story Protocol, Xion, 0G Labs, ZetaChain, Omni Network, Plume, Hooked Protocol, and a graveyard of L1s and L2s whose names I misplaced on purpose. I showed up, ran the thing, helped the newbies, made fun of the tension, and left the timeline cleaner than I found it. Allegedly.
